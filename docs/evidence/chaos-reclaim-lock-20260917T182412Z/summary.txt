(b) the frozen state.db writer (PLT-4646):
  - `stale_owner_frozen_in_transaction` (new): gateway A stops itself inside a write transaction
    (TSLS_STORE_FREEZE_FLAG failpoint, 20.6-35.4 s, site recorded as
    crates/application/src/repository/sqlite/triggers.rs:522). Nobody reclaims anybody while the
    lock is held; after SIGCONT B takes its lease back (1 renewal each run), is never fenced and
    never reclaimed, and reclaims A; A notices its own stall, fences itself, refuses new invokes
    with 503 and reclaims nobody; the invocation is settled exactly once and never overwritten.
  - `db_locked_past_lease` (state.db locked for 12 s > lease 6 s + skew): the gateway now serves
    again without a restart (`recovery.serves_without_restart`), where the 2026-09-17 runs
    recorded `recovery.fenced_itself_after_losing_its_lease`.
  - `stale_owner_sync_lease`, `sync_gateway_kill`, `orphan_recovery_after_crash`, `baseline`,
    `db_locked_within_lease`: unchanged behaviour, all pass.

RESIDUAL: a request that makes several store calls can still take longer than one store call's
~10 s bound while the lock is held (`outage.b_sync_while_a_frozen`: 503 Host.StoreUnavailable
after 5.3 s in two runs, no answer within the 20 s client timeout in the third) — the known
limitation in docs/failure-matrix.md §6.2.
