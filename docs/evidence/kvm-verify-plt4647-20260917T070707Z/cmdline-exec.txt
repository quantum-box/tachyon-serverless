cmdline.py forks a child that execs /usr/bin/sleep 0.02 and reads /proc/<pid>/cmdline and
/proc/<pid>/stat in a tight loop until the child is a zombie. It counts reads that came back EMPTY
while the process was not a zombie and before the new image's argv ("sleep") had been seen,
i.e. while the execve was in progress.
