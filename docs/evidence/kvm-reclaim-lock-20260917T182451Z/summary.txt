  While A is stopped nobody reclaims anybody (outage.nobody_reclaimed_while_the_store_was_locked).
  After SIGCONT: B (which kept running) takes its lease back — `dispatcher lease renewed after the
  store was unavailable`, lease.b_is_not_fenced ok, 1 renewal — and reclaims A; A detects its own

  invocation is settled exactly once and never overwritten afterwards.
