FINDINGS
1. Harness bugs found and fixed on the way (not product issues):
   - OOM scenario aborted under pipefail when one of two console directories did not exist (run1, both
     attempts) -> fixed in 360dbcb, passed in run2 and run3.
   - orphan_recovery_after_crash run1 attempt 1: setup.state_to_recover saw 0 orphan objects (the object
     put + SIGKILL failpoint left none that time; attempt 2 and runs 2-3 had 1). Recorded, not investigated.
2. Host usage of environments ended on crash / fencing paths (Firecracker only; product gap, not changed):
   - an environment the reclaim terminated first and its old owner settled later (stale owner), or one
     abandoned while booting during a graceful shutdown (orphan scenario, reason Shutdown), gets its
     EnvironmentStopped with cgroup_cpu_usec / memory.peak = `unknown` (the cgroup was already gone when the
     sample was read). Never an invented value.
   - an environment terminated by the reclaim / startup reconcile of a dead owner gets NO EnvironmentStopped
     (ReconcileService does not meter): run3 sync_gateway_kill 4 of 7 terminal environments, orphan 6 of 14,
     stale owner 4 of 11 have no stop event. Their host cost (the whole microVM lifetime) is not metered.
     Attempt-level usage (AttemptSettled) and "each event once" (cv.usage_counted_once) held in every run.
   The harness now requires provider_reported-or-unknown in those two scenarios and records the counts
   (usage.* observations); the other scenarios still require cgroup usage on every stop (commit 40a3c4a).
3. stale_owner_sync_lease in run3 failed both attempts the same way: after gateway A was SIGSTOPped, gateway
   B could not write state.db ("database is locked" / "busy for 5 s", gateway-b.log from 5 s after the stop
   until A's SIGCONT 2 min later), so B could not heartbeat, reclaim, take the scheduler lease or refresh its
   config; B's own leases expired (Host.AuthLeaseExpired, 503) and A, resumed, completed the invocation it
   still owned (running -> succeeded, which is correct because nothing had reclaimed it). The same scenario
   passed all reclaim/fencing checks in run1 (2 attempts) and run2 (B reclaimed after lease + skew, fenced
   A's VMM terminated and its jail/cgroup removed, late completion refused, A fenced, B serving).
   Reading: A was frozen while holding the SQLite write lock of the shared state.db; with one SQLite file
   shared by two processes a frozen writer stops every other writer, and lease/fencing cannot help. Not
   reproduced deterministically and the transaction A was in was not identified (its last log lines were an
   object retention pass / an async settle). Recorded as a limitation of the shared-SQLite two-gateway
   setup (ADR-0003 is single host), not fixed.
4. Secret scan: 0 files with the secret value in every scenario of every run, across the scratch dir and
   /srv/jailer.
